# Italian (it) glossary

Conquest progression: campaign journal = diario; transport berth = posto di trasporto;
veteran berth = posto per veterani; deploy = schierare; in reserve = in riserva.
Compatibility playback = riproduzione compatibile.

Register: the player is addressed with "tu". Capitals keep accents (È, À, Ì, Ò, Ù).
Foreign unit names from the game data are treated as masculine (Italian default for loanwords);
where a gender would clash, "un'unità {unit}" or no article is used.

## Career ranks (gradi, `ta_config::profiles::RANKS`, one every five levels)

The Esercito Italiano's grades, in order. Italy has nothing between Soldato and Caporale, so
those two come from the Swiss Army's Italian ranks (Appuntato, Appuntato capo). Staff Sergeant
is Sergente maggiore by grade (OR-6: sergente maggiore is a false friend of Sergeant Major, an
OR-9, here Primo maresciallo). Level = livello ("Prossimo grado: {rank} al livello {level}");
promoted = Promozione / PROMOZIONE; service star = stella di servizio (STELLE DI SERVIZIO).

## Conquista: i talenti del Comandante (`conquest.rs`, `conquest_ui/perks.rs`)

A perk is a talento (pl. talenti): not vantaggio, which is IT BRINGS (VANTAGGIO) over a system's
reward, nor abilità (the units' abilities). The perks' names are roles of the Esercito: geniere
(combat engineer, as Battlefield's engineer class), furiere (quartermaster). "half as fast again"
has no Italian idiom: il 50% più velocemente / il 50% in più; "a quarter more/harder" = un quarto
di … in più. Straight apostrophe ('), as the other Conquest strings.
