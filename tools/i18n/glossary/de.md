# German (de) glossary: TA Reborn interface

Conquest progression: campaign journal = Journal; transport berth = Transportplatz;
veteran berth = Veteranenplatz; deploy = einsetzen; in reserve = in Reserve.
Compatibility playback = Kompatibilitätswiedergabe.

Voice: du; terse. Imperative du-form for instructions ("Klicke", "Wähle"); infinitive for
tooltips that say what a click does ("Klicken, um ..."). Capitals stay capitals (ß -> SS).
Key names stay English (Ctrl, Shift, Alt, Esc, Escape, Space, Backspace, Tab, F1-F12).
Unit and map names from the game data stay English (Kbot Lab, Peewee, Metal Extractor,
Solar Collector, Lugers, Zeus, Krogoth Gantry, Painted Desert, Lava Run...).
Avoid articles before {unit}: "Zerstöre {unit}", "Baue {unit}".
Counts: "Einheiten: {n}" where n may be 1.
Campaign "Fail if {goal}" = "Niederlage, wenn {goal}" (goals are verb-final clauses);
scenario "Lost if: {goal}" = "Niederlage: {goal}" (goals are main clauses).
"Nothing to {order} there" = "Dort lässt sich nichts {order}" (order = lowercased verb label).
Scenario goal phrases are accusative objects of "Zerstöre {what}", "Verliere {what} nicht",
"Bringe {what} zum markierten Punkt"; "all your units" (only in the lose frame) = "deine letzte
Einheit", since "Verliere alle deine Einheiten nicht" says the wrong thing.

## Career ranks (`ta_config::profiles::RANKS`, one every five levels)

Bundeswehr names, in order. Corporal was already Gefreiter and Lieutenant Leutnant, so the steps
the Bundeswehr has none of below them come from the Wehrmacht (Obersoldat, Oberschütze: its
OR-2, as Private First Class and Lance Corporal are both E-3) and the NVA (Unterleutnant).
Level = Stufe ("Nächster Rang: {rank} ab Stufe {level}"); promoted = befördert (BEFÖRDERT);
service star = Dienststern (DIENSTSTERNE); service record = Dienstakte.

## Conquest: the Commander's perks (`conquest.rs`, `conquest_ui/perks.rs`)

A perk is a Vorteil, as in Cyberpunk 2077's German (not Extra: EXTRAS is the menu's page; not
Fähigkeit: the units' abilities). The perks' names are role nouns (Pionier is the Bundeswehr's
