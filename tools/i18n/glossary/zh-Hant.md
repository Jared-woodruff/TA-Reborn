# zh-Hant glossary (繁體中文, Taiwan usage)

Style: 你 (not 您); full-width ，。：；？！「」（） in sentences; keep `·` `×` `/` `–` `…`;
em dash in sentences is ——; a half-width space between CJK and Latin text, numbers or
placeholders that hold them (`{n} 個單位`, `D 砲`), none next to full-width punctuation.
Unit and map names from the game data stay English (Kbot Lab, Peewee, Solar Collector, Painted Desert).
Arm / Core / ARM / CORE, TA, TA Reborn, Total Annihilation, expansion titles stay English.
