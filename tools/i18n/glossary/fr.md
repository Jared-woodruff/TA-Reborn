# French (fr) glossary: TA Reborn

Conquest progression: campaign journal = journal; transport berth = place de transport;
veteran berth = place de vétéran; deploy = déployer; in reserve = en réserve.
Compatibility playback = lecture de compatibilité.

Typography: no-break space (U+00A0) before `:` `;` `?` `!` and inside « », and as the
thousands separator (15 000). Exception: the classic panel's lines drawn in TA's bitmap font
(`hud_classic.rs` "Kills: {n}", "Losses: {n}", "Units: {n}...") use a plain space.
Player addressed as "vous". Buttons/captions in capitals keep accents (É, À, Ç).
Unit and map names (Peewee, Kbot Lab, Solar Collector, Painted Desert...) stay in English:
they come from the English game data. "l'unité {unit}" avoids le/la/l' before a unit name.

## Career ranks (grades, `ta_config::profiles::RANKS`, one every five levels)

The French Army's grades, in order (Major was already the OF-3, Commandant being the Commander).
France has nothing between soldat de 1re classe and caporal, so Lance Corporal is caporal
suppléant (its attested French name); Warrant Officer is aspirant, the step between adjudant-chef
and sous-lieutenant. Level = niveau; rank = grade ("Grade suivant : {rank} au niveau {level}",
"au-delà du plus haut grade"); promoted = Promotion / PROMOTION (not the gendered promu);
service star = étoile de service (ÉTOILES DE SERVICE).
