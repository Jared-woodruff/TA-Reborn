# Japanese (ja) glossary

Register: interface sentences polite です/ます; labels, buttons, captions short nouns or
dictionary-form verbs (戻る, 続ける). Objectives in dictionary form (〜を破壊する).
Lore (timeline) in literary plain form; scenario briefings as plain military orders
(〜せよ); chatter by temper: Arm officer formal (貴官), Core AI clinical (貴様, noun
endings), mercenary casual (お前, 俺).
Punctuation: 、。：？！「」（） in Japanese text; keep · × / … – → % and key names.
Unit, map and planet names stay in English (they appear in English in game).
