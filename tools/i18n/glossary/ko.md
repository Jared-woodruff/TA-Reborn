# Korean (ko) glossary — TA Reborn

Register: UI prompts and help in polite 하세요/합니다; scenario briefings in formal
하십시오/합니다 (HQ addressing 사령관님); lore (timeline) in narrative 해라체 (~했다);
labels, buttons, captions as short nouns. No particle directly after a {placeholder}
(reworded, or invariant particles only: 의, 에, 에서, 에게, 까지, 부터, 입니다, 인).

Counters: units 기 (유닛 {n}기), unit types (archives, mod catalogue) 종 (유닛 {n}종),
players 명, systems/maps/things 개, battles 회, minutes 분, seconds 초, per-second rates /초.

Shared keys checked against their call sites: FULL (storage tag + water reflections level)
= 최대; LARGE (photo size + conquest galaxy size) = 대형; HEIGHT (editor map size + height
overlay) = 높이, so terrain heights are 높이 everywhere (ALT in the cockpit is 고도);
READY (lobby tag + cockpit weapon state) = 준비 완료; REPLAY (briefing button, HUD caption,
saved-games tag) = 리플레이; PLAY (launcher + jukebox) = 플레이; N/E/S/W are only the
cockpit compass = 북/동/남/서 (key names never go through t()).

Never translated: Arm/Core (ARM/CORE), TA, TA Reborn, Total Annihilation, Cavedog, GOG,
ModDB, Steam, Windows, key names, +chat commands, file names, unit and map names (Kbot Lab,
Peewee, Metal Extractor, Solar Collector, Luger, Painted Desert...), planet names in lore
(Empyrrean, Hydross...), expansion titles (The Core Contingency, Battle Tactics,
Commander Pack).

상병, so 병장 has no place, and Lance Corporal, between 일병 and 상병, is 상병 대리 (acting
corporal).

## The Galactic War page: chronicle and archive (`lore.rs`, `timeline.rs`, `timeline/galaxy.rs`)

The chronicle in narrative 해라체. Each archive document has its own voice: CORE's edict and
directive legal 해라체 (~하여야 한다, ~한다); the ARM's declaration dry 합쇼체 to 귀하들 (정중히
사양하겠습니다); the field manual and primer 해라체 to 귀관/제군 (~하라); the letter in a young
trooper's 해요체 (나, 당신); the machine's memory plain 해라체. CORE's signals, all capitals in
English, are 개조식 (~함, ~할 것, 허용 범위 내). Years have no thousands separator (3977년,
212–3977년). Headings keep Latin names in capitals (EMPYRREAN의 배양조, 위협 평가: KROGOTH); prose
in mixed case. `[[...]]` (blacked-out words) are translated inside the brackets, with no particle
after them (ARM의 손실: [[수십억]]).
