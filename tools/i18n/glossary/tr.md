CAPITALS: i -> İ, ı -> I. Placeholders never take suffixes: reword with a colon
("Yok et: {what}"), a following noun that carries the suffix ("{unit} birimini"),
a postposition ("{player} için", "{time} boyunca") or a passive/nominative subject.
Percent before the number: %{pct}. Seconds "sn", minutes "dk".

## Achievements

A tier is the badge's metal (kademe). "alone" = tek başına (no allied player at all), "in one
battle" = tek bir savaşta, "on Hard" = Zor düzeyinde; numbers grouped 1.000, 1.000.000.

units' veterancy ranks above are another list; only Recruit, Acemi, is shared). TSK has no
warrant officers, so Staff Sergeant to Warrant Officer follow its own NCO ladder in order.
